## Task Type: Text-to-Video (T2V) for Google Flow Veo 3

This is a Text-to-Video generation task building the entire visual narrative and audio soundscape from text.

Guidelines:
- Establish the scene setting, subject, lighting, and action with vivid sensory detail.
- Camera movement MUST be written as its own separate sentence.
- If dialogue is included, format strictly as: Character says: "..." (no subtitles)
- Conclude with Audio:, SFX:, Music:, and Negative: lines.
