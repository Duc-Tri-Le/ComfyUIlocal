## Task Type: First/Last Frame Chaining (FL2V) for Google Flow Veo 3

This is a First-and-Last Frame video generation task bridging the starting image to the final ending image.

Guidelines:
- Begin precisely with the initial frame's composition, subject pose, and lighting.
- Describe the seamless, organic kinetic trajectory and camera choreography bridging into the final frame's composition.
- Write camera movement as a dedicated separate sentence.
- If dialogue is included, format strictly as: Character says: "..." (no subtitles)
- Conclude with Audio:, SFX:, Music:, and Negative: lines.
